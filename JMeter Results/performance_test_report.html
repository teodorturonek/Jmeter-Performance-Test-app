Performance Test Report

How the Task App Coped: Normal Load vs Heavy Load

Normal run: 5 users at once, 105 requests over about 2 minutes  •  Heavy run: 50 users at once, 1,050 requests over about 7 minutes

1The Short Version

Under normal load the app worked well: every request succeeded and pages answered in about 0.16 seconds. Under heavy load it failed. The average answer took 3.0 seconds, well over our 2-second limit, and almost 1 in 5 requests (18.1%) failed. On this evidence the app is not ready for busy, real-world traffic.

4What This Means, in Plain English

What went well

With a small group of 5 people using it at once, the app did everything right. All 105 requests succeeded and every action finished in well under half a second (about 0.16 seconds on average). Logging out stayed quick and dependable even under heavy pressure. Getting the security token stayed under 1 second on average, though it did sometimes fail.

What struggled under heavy load

When we raised the load to 50 people at once, the app slowed down sharply and started failing. Searching for tasks was the worst: it took 6.4 seconds on average, and almost half of all searches (46.7%) failed. Creating, updating and deleting tasks slowed to between 3 and 4.2 seconds, and between 14% and 25% of those requests failed. Even logging in took 2.4 seconds and failed 12% of the time.

What a real user would have experienced

A real person would have watched the screen spin for several seconds after most clicks. Sometimes they would have seen an error message telling them the system was busy or had broken. About 1 in 5 of their actions would not have worked at all, so tasks they created or changed might not have been saved. That leads to frustration, repeated clicks (which add even more load) and calls to the help desk.

Is the app ready for real users?

Not yet. It is fine for a handful of people, but it cannot handle 50 people using it at the same time. Before launch, the team should fix the search, the server errors and the capacity limits listed below. Then the heavy load test should be run again to prove the fixes work.

5Top 3 Things to Fix

🔴

Priority 1: Search Tasks breaks under load

Problem: Under heavy load, Search Tasks averaged 6.4 seconds, failed 46.7% of the time and hit the 10-second time limit 31 times.

Fix: Profile the search query, then add database indexes, limit results per page and cache common searches so each search does far less work.

🟡

Priority 2: The server crashes and runs out of room

Problem: There were 121 "server broke" (500) and "server too busy" (503) errors, mostly when creating, updating or deleting tasks.

Fix: Check the server logs behind the 500 errors. Then add capacity (more app instances, larger database connection pools) so the system can serve 50 people at once.

🟢

Priority 3: Real users are being turned away by rate limits

Problem: 69 requests were rejected with "too many requests" (429), even though this was ordinary user activity.

Fix: Set the rate limit to match expected real-world traffic. Also add automatic "wait and retry" handling so users don't see an error.
